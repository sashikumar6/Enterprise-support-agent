# Ticketmaster sample image review

Reviewed: 2026-08-13 (America/New_York)

Nine images were reviewed visually: the first three sanitized samples from Boston, New York,
and Philadelphia.

- Seven were specific artist or event photographs.
- Two were generic but relevant thematic images (abstract lights and a musical instrument).
- None were missing, broken, offensive, or unusable for an event card.

Conclusion: image coverage is suitable for the initial product, but URL-based checks cannot
reliably distinguish event-specific art from generic provider art. Scout must tolerate generic
and missing imagery and must not claim that every image uniquely depicts the listed event.
